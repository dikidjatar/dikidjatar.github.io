Privacy Policy for AI Files

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

AI Files ("the App") is a local and cloud file manager for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how the App accesses, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Access and How We Use It

  • Device Storage & Files: The App requests storage permissions solely to browse, read, create, edit, move, copy, archive, and delete files on your device as directed by you.
  • Google Drive Data (OAuth2): When you choose to connect your Google Drive account, the App requests access to your Google account email, basic profile name, storage quota, and Google Drive files (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive). This data is used strictly to display and manage your Drive files and folders directly inside the App.
  • FTP / SFTP Servers: Server addresses, usernames, passwords, or SSH keys you enter are used solely to establish direct connections from your device to your chosen remote servers.
  • AI Assistant (Optional): If you configure your own Gemini API key and invoke the AI assistant, only the prompts and file contents you explicitly choose to inspect are sent directly from your device to the configured AI API endpoint.

2. Data Storage, Sharing, and Security

  • No Developer Servers or Analytics: The App does not operate a backend server, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not collect analytics or tracking data.
  • Local Encrypted Credentials: OAuth access tokens, FTP/SFTP credentials, and API keys are stored locally on your device using Android Keystore encryption (AES-256-GCM) and are never transmitted to the developer.
  • No Sale or Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or file contents with third parties.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

AI Files' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Drive data is never used for advertising, profiling, or training AI/ML models.

4. Data Retention and Revoking Access

You can remove any connected storage or clear credentials at any time inside the App's storage settings, or uninstall the App to delete all locally stored data. You may also revoke Google Drive access at any time from your Google Account Permissions page.
